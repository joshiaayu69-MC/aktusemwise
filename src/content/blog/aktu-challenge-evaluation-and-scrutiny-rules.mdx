Students dissatisfied with their semester exam scores can utilize AKTU's two-stage challenge
evaluation and scrutiny system. AKTU provides a transparent digital evaluation process allowing
students to personally view their evaluated answer booklet before requesting a complete re-check.

## Stage 1: Viewing Evaluated Answer Sheet (Scrutiny)

Before challenging an evaluation, a candidate must apply for Stage 1:

- **Application Window**: Typically opens within 10 to 15 days of official result declaration on AKTU ERP.
- **Stage 1 Fee**: Approximately ₹300 per subject.
- **Process**: Upon payment verification, the university generates a secure digital link on the student dashboard allowing access to the scanned, evaluated answer booklet with examiner markings and question-by-question totals.
- **Action**: Check for totaling errors, un-evaluated answers, or discrepancies against official AKTU model answers.

## Stage 2: Challenge Evaluation (Re-evaluation by Two Independent Examiners)

If you identify significant grading mistakes in your answer booklet:

- **Eligibility**: Only candidates who completed Stage 1 can apply for Stage 2 challenge evaluation.
- **Stage 2 Fee**: Approximately ₹2,500 per subject.
- **Evaluation Mechanism**: The booklet is forwarded to two independent external evaluators without revealing previous marks.
- **Calculation of New Marks**: The average of the two independent re-evaluations is computed as the revised score.

## University Fee Refund Policy

AKTU maintains a progressive refund policy:

- If your revised marks increase by **20% or more** of the maximum theory marks compared to the original score, the university refunds ₹1,000 to ₹1,500 of the challenge evaluation fee back into the candidate's verified bank account.
- The revised marks (whether increased or decreased beyond established threshold rules) become final and replace the previous score on the AKTU One View portal.
