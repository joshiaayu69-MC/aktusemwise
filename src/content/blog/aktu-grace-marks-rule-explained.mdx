AKTU awards grace marks to candidates who are falling short by a small margin to pass an academic
year or clear degree requirements. Understanding the official ordinance helps students plan carry-over
appearances strategically without unnecessary panic.

## Maximum Grace Marks Allowed

Under the standard B.Tech ordinance:

- Up to **7 to 10 grace marks** (depending on the applicable scheme and admission batch) can be awarded in an academic year to clear a maximum of up to 2 subjects.
- Grace marks are awarded only to enable a student to reach the minimum external theory passing threshold (typically 21 marks out of 70, or 30 out of 100).
- Grace marks are granted only if the student has satisfied all other semester passing criteria (such as minimum aggregate sessional + external marks).

## What Does "PWG" (Pass With Grace) Mean?

When grace marks are applied to your result:

- The subject status displays **PWG (Pass With Grace)** on the AKTU One View marksheet.
- The marksheet indicates the original marks scored followed by the grace increment.
- You are not required to appear for a Carry-Over Paper (COP) for that subject.

## Does Grace Marks Affect Your Final Degree Division?

1. **Award of First Division**: In standard AKTU guidelines, students who graduate with PWG status may be ineligible for "First Division with Honours / Distinction" even if their overall CGPA is 7.5 or higher.
2. **First Division Standard**: Standard First Division (CGPA $\ge 6.50$) is generally awarded if the overall cumulative grade point average meets the threshold, provided no active backlogs exist.
3. **Surrendering Grace Marks**: If a student wishes to improve their marks and remove the "PWG" tag from their official transcript, AKTU provides an option to appear in the carry-over examination. Scoring above passing marks in the COP replaces the grace marks and restores normal passing status.
