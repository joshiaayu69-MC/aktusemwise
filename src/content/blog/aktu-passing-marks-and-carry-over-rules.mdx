Navigating AKTU examinations requires understanding the two-tier passing criteria: the individual
external theory minimum and the overall aggregate course threshold. Falling below either results
in a Carry-Over Paper (COP) or back paper.

## 1. External Theory 30% Cutoff Rule

For every end-semester university theory examination:

- **70-Mark Question Papers** (current 2022-23 scheme): A student must secure a minimum of **21 marks (30%)** in the end-semester written examination.
- **100-Mark Question Papers** (older schemes): A student must secure a minimum of **30 marks (30%)** in the external written paper.
- If you score below 21 out of 70, you receive a carry-over in that subject regardless of having high sessional marks (even if you scored 30/30 in internal assessment).

## 2. Overall Aggregate 40% Subject Threshold

To successfully clear a credit subject:

- Total Marks = Internal Sessional (30) + External Theory (70) = 100 Marks.
- The combined total must be at least **40 marks (40%)** to be awarded a passing letter grade (Grade P or higher).
- Example: If a student scores 21/70 in external exam and 19/30 in sessional, Total = 40/100 -> **PASS**.
- If a student scores 21/70 in external exam and 18/30 in sessional, Total = 39/100 -> Carry-Over or eligible for 1 grace mark if permitted.

## 3. Promotion & Year-Back (Carry-Over) Limits

Under AKTU CBCS rules:

- To be promoted from 1st year to 2nd year, or from 2nd year to 3rd year, students must clear at least **50% of the total registered credits** in the combined odd and even semesters of that academic year.
- If a student fails to accumulate 50% credits across both semesters of the year, they face a **Year Back (YB)** and cannot register for higher semester subjects until sufficient credits are cleared via special or carry-over examinations.
