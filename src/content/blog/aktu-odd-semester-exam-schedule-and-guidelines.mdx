Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow conducts end-semester examinations
in two phases for odd semester academic sessions. Phase 1 covers senior semesters (regular and
carry-over exams for 5th, 7th, and 9th semesters along with postgraduate programs), while Phase 2
is conducted for 1st and 3rd semesters.

## Key Exam Guidelines & Phase Breakdown

The examination controller releases official circulars and tentative center lists on the AKTU ERP
portal. Here is what students need to verify prior to examination commencement:

1. **Admit Card Verification**:
   - Admit cards must be downloaded through student ERP credentials (`erp.aktu.ac.in`).
   - Confirm your photographed likeness, signature, assigned examination center code, and registered subject codes (including carry-over papers if registered).
   - Get the admit card attested by your college Director or Principal with the official seal if instructed on the circular.

2. **Phase 1 vs Phase 2 Schedule**:
   - **Phase 1**: B.Tech 3rd and 4th year (Semesters 5 and 7), B.Pharm, MBA, MCA final semesters.
   - **Phase 2**: B.Tech 1st year (Semester 1) and B.Tech 2nd year (Semester 3) regular and COP exams.

3. **Examination Center Protocol**:
   - Report to the designated nodal exam center at least 45 minutes before commencement.
   - Carry original college ID card and printed admit card.
   - Digital gadgets, programmable calculators, smart watches, and unauthorized paper sheets are strictly prohibited inside the hall. Non-programmable scientific calculators are permitted for computational engineering subjects (like Mathematics, Fluid Mechanics, Structural Analysis).

## Sessional & Practical Marks Submission

Before the theory examinations, affiliated institutes submit internal marks (CT-1, CT-2, Teacher
Assessment, and Practical Internal) via the AKTU AMS (Academic Management System) portal.
Students should verify their sessional attendance and internal marks with departmental proctors
before the university freezing deadline.
